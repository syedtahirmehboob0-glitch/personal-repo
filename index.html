A few helpful details

Good to know.

How do I place an order?

Add items to your bag and send an order request on WhatsApp. We will confirm stock, final total, delivery charge and your address before placing the supplier order. Your request is not confirmed until we reply.

Where do you deliver?

We currently take requests for delivery within Pakistan. The supplier lists 3–5 business days for these products; timing depends on city and courier. We confirm the estimate and delivery charge with you before dispatch.

Can I pay cash on delivery?

Cash on delivery is available through the supplier's listed service, subject to address and stock confirmation. No payment is taken on this website.

What if there is a problem with my order?

Message us as soon as your parcel arrives. Eligible incorrect, incomplete or damaged orders may be returnable under the supplier's policy; we will confirm the applicable terms before submitting your order.
